## Background & problem

To have an LLM summarise a meeting, you first need a transcript that says who said what. Cloud services mean uploading the recording to a third party, and their Chinese output is often Simplified or uses mainland phrasing.

## My role

Built it alone: the transcription pipeline, the API, the web UI and the setup guide.

## Approach & technical decisions

FunASR chains four models: voice activity detection, speech recognition, punctuation and speaker diarization. They load onto the GPU once and are shared across jobs. OpenCC then converts the text to Taiwanese Traditional Chinese.

The output format is designed for LLMs. Consecutive turns by the same speaker are merged and timestamps are kept coarse, which cuts about 80% of the formatting tokens compared with line-per-segment Markdown. The service is FastAPI with a single-file web UI that needs no build step, and can be protected with a bearer token or Cloudflare Access.

## Outcome

Runs end to end on a single RTX 3090. The source is on GitHub.
