title: Focus Moment
summary: Third prize at the Cross-Strait Youth Maker Competition. A classroom system that uses image processing and AI to estimate attention without any wearables, giving teachers real-time feedback on their pacing.
---

## Background & problem

From the front of the room, a teacher can't watch every student at once, and often learns that a topic didn't land only when the exam results come in.

## My role

Team member, working on the system's design and development.

## Approach & technical decisions

The system analyses classroom video to estimate how focused students are, with nothing for them to wear, and gives the teacher feedback in real time.

## Outcome

Third prize at the Cross-Strait Youth Maker Competition.

## What I learned

When you analyse people from video, accuracy is only half the job. Privacy, and how it feels to be analysed, has to be designed in from the start.
