## Background & problem

A club bulk-buys OpenAI and Anthropic subscriptions and splits the cost across members. Tracking every month in a spreadsheet who paid, who hasn't and whether the screenshots match quickly becomes a job nobody wants.

## My role

Designed and built it alone: the data model, the Discord bot, the backend API, two frontends, tests and deployment.

## Approach & technical decisions

The whole payment loop moved into Discord, where members already are. A member taps **Pay**, picks a payment channel and is done. One submission settles every subscription they have for the period, and members who haven't linked their account pick their name in the same flow.

The backend is a single Cloudflare Worker with the ledger in D1 and payment screenshots in a private R2 bucket. A daily cron opens billing periods, sends overdue reminders and enforces screenshot retention. Every notification is deduplicated through a log table, so re-runs never double-send.

A few deliberate choices:

- **Amounts are frozen per period**, so a price change never rewrites past bills.
- **Core and channel adapters are separate.** Billing logic doesn't depend on Discord, and LINE and Telegram adapters are pre-wired.
- **The admin site sits behind Cloudflare Access.** The SPA and API share an origin, and the Worker re-verifies the JWT and an email allow-list.
- **CSV imports preview the diff first**: which subscriptions get added, paused or reactivated. Nothing is written until you confirm, and re-runs are idempotent.
