## Background & problem

Litter in a city's corners often goes unreported, and people who want to help don't know where clean-up is needed or who else would join.

## My role

I built the iOS app for the team, including sign-in, events and the community features.

## Approach & technical decisions

The app is SwiftUI with an MVVM structure, using Firebase for sign-in, data and image storage. Users create clean-up events with photos, and others browse event cards, like them and sign up.
