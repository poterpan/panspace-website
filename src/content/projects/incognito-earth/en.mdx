title: Incognito Earth
summary: Honorable mention at the Mobile Application Innovation Contest. A community app for cleaner cities that lets people report litter, see it on a map, and start or join local clean-up events.
---

## Background & problem

Litter in a city's corners often goes unreported, and people who want to help don't know where clean-up is needed or who else would join.

## My role

I built the iOS app for the team, including sign-in, events and the community features.

## Approach & technical decisions

The app is SwiftUI with an MVVM structure, using Firebase for sign-in, data and image storage. Users create clean-up events with photos, and others browse event cards, like them and sign up.

## Outcome

Honorable mention at the Mobile Application Innovation Contest. The source is on GitHub.

## What I learned

In a community app, the data model decides most things. Once the relationships between events, photos, likes and sign-ups were clear, the screens and sync logic followed.
