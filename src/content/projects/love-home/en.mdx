## Background & problem

In dual-income families, parents have limited time with their kids, and conversations shrink to "is your homework done?" We wanted an app that both sides would open and that gives them a reason to interact.

## My role

I built the iOS app for the team.

## Approach & technical decisions

The app is SwiftUI with accounts and data in Firebase. Parents and children each pick a role when they sign up and then pair into one family.

The core is a raising game: as the family completes missions together, their pet monster grows level by level. Sharing covers photos, voice recordings and posts, plus small touches like shake-to-interact.
